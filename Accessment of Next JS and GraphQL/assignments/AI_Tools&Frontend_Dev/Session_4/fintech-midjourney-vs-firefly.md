## 3. Three Key Visual Differences Observed

### **Difference 1: Cinematic Lighting & Photorealism vs. Crisp Vector Graphic Aesthetics**
* **Midjourney:** Generates highly cinematic, dark-mode lighting with complex reflections, realistic glassmorphism depth, and metallic ray-tracing highlights on gold coins and credit cards. It creates a high-end, premium wealth management feel.
* **Adobe Firefly:** Generates clean, bright, corporate-friendly vector 3D illustrations with flat soft shadows. Firefly’s output resembles commercial tech illustrations (like Stripe, Revolut, or Wise marketing graphics).

### **Difference 2: Text Rendering & UI Dashboard Realism**
* **Midjourney:** Renders abstract, futuristic holographic charts with dense visual detail, but numbers and text strings on the credit card and charts appear partially stylized or garbled ("micro-text artifacting").
* **Adobe Firefly:** Excels at rendering sharp, readable UI elements. The credit card text ("GLOBAL BANK"), phone mockups, numbers (`$14,750.30`), pie charts, and navigation tabs are crisp, legible, and structurally aligned like real web UI components.

### **Difference 3: Subject Composition & Commercial Usability for Web Development**
* **Midjourney:** Produces dramatic, artistic compositions with heavy background bokeh and dark environmental lighting. While visually stunning, it requires adding dark gradient overlays to achieve readable text contrast for landing page headlines.
* **Adobe Firefly:** Leaves clean, un-cluttered negative space on the left side of the 16:9 banner specifically designed for placing H1 hero text and CTA buttons ("Open Account Today"), making it immediately usable for front-end web development without additional editing.
