# Task 4: Keyword Clustering for Swiggy-Style Food Delivery Website

## What is Keyword Clustering?
Keyword clustering is an SEO strategy of grouping semantically related keywords (e.g., "late night pizza delivery", "best pizza near me", "24/7 food ordering app") into a single, cohesive content cluster targeted by a core web page, rather than creating separate weak pages for every individual keyword variation.

---

## 3-Sentence Explanation: How Keyword Clustering Helps Swiggy Rank Higher

1. **Builds Strong Topical Authority:** By grouping closely related food queries (such as cuisine names, meal types, and location tags) onto specialized landing pages, a Swiggy-style platform establishes deep topical authority that satisfies search engine intent algorithms like Google Hummingbird and RankBrain.
2. **Prevents Keyword Cannibalization:** Clustering prevents multiple internal dish or restaurant pages from competing against each other for identical search queries, consolidating organic link equity and search authority onto high-converting category pages.
3. **Maximizes Organic Search Traffic Coverage:** Instead of ranking for just one isolated search term, a single keyword-clustered landing page can simultaneously rank for dozens of long-tail variations (e.g., "fastest food delivery", "order biryani online", "late night snacks near me"), dramatically increasing organic search visibility and click-through rates.
