## Budget Smartphone Buyer’s Checklist for 2024

Before making your final purchase, keep these three golden rules in mind:

1. **Insist on 5G Band Support:** Ensure the device supports at least 7 to 9 NSA/SA 5G frequency bands to guarantee seamless network compatibility with Jio True 5G and Airtel 5G Plus.
2. **Prioritize Storage Type:** Look for UFS 2.2 storage over older eMMC 5.1 storage for significantly faster app launch times and file transfer speeds.
3. **Software Update Guarantee:** Check if the manufacturer promises at least 2 years of Android OS upgrades and 3 years of security patches.
