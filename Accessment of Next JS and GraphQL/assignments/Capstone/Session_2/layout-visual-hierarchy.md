## Visual Hierarchy Analysis: Zomato & E-Commerce Dashboard Layouts

In analyzing the Zomato and E-Commerce dashboard wireframes, visual weight is strategically distributed to guide user attention through an intuitive, action-oriented hierarchy. 
The **primary focal point** of the layout is dominated by high-contrast action elements—specifically the persistent **"Place Order / Book Now"** CTA buttons rendered in vibrant brand colors (`#E23744` red and `#FF9F00` orange), paired with bold total pricing indicators that immediately signal purchase readiness. 
The **secondary level of hierarchy** is occupied by the central content cards, such as featured restaurant food photos and active promo discount banners, which utilize larger font sizes and card shadows to draw second-look engagement. 
**Tertiary elements** include the top navigation bar, category filter chips, and subtle metadata text (delivery times, ratings, and vehicle numbers), which remain visually subdued in neutral gray tones so they do not compete with main purchasing actions. 
Furthermore, spatial grouping and generous padding around primary cards establish clear visual relationships, allowing users to scan key information in seconds without cognitive overload. 
Ultimately, this deliberate progression from vibrant primary buttons down to subtle filter pills creates a frictionless user experience that directly maximizes user conversion rates.
